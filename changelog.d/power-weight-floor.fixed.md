The power-weight (endpoint) table of a z-varying modal operator measures its round-off floor at 32 positions across the span instead of three central ones, and relaxes its tolerance by decades if its interval budget is exhausted anyway, as the dispersion table does; a table still exhausted at the loosest tolerance is logged as one whose accuracy is not stated. Along a fill whose densities vary node by node the floor differs from interval to interval; three probes saw a twelfth of the worst, and one interval spent the whole budget, most of a translation's time (LupoAirOsc's six-species air cell: 14.8 s to 3.8 s). The table stays exact to round-off either way.
