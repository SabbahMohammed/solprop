The power-weight (endpoint) table of a z-varying modal operator measures its round-off floor at 32 positions across the span instead of three central ones, and relaxes its tolerance by decades if its interval budget is exhausted anyway, as the dispersion table does; a table still exhausted at the loosest tolerance is logged as one whose accuracy is not stated. Along a fill whose densities vary node by node the floor differs from interval to interval: on a six-species air fill varying along z, three central probes read 2.3e-11 and the 32 across the span 2.6e-9, about a hundred times more. At the tolerance the three set, the table spent its whole interval budget (747 intervals), most of a translation's time; it now resolves at 2.1e-8 in 292 intervals, and the translation runs three to four times faster. The table stays exact to round-off either way.
