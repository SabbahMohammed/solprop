Added `loss="gas"` to the capillary (`MarcatiliMode`) and tube-lattice (`VincettiMode`) modes and to `prop_capillary`/`Simulation.from_capillary`: the fill's own absorption is kept and the guide's loss removed, an ideal wall for the capillary and no confinement term for the lattice, so that an absorbing trace gas such as ozone in air attenuates the light while the wall does not ([gas absorption without the wall](docs/guide/waveguides/capillary-model.md#gas-absorption-without-the-wall)). The rectangular, planar and tabulated modes refuse it by name.
