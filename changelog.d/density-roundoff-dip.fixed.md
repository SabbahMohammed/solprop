A density path that touches zero no longer has its translation refused when its fitted table dips below zero by rounding: a curve meeting a zero end with zero slope, or an unresolved interval ringing about a vacuum, left the table about 1e-18 of its scale below zero, and the domain check refused the run. `profile_table` now lifts each dipping interval by its dip when every dip is within 64 ulp of the table's largest value. It does so for every scalar path it tabulates, not only densities: a pre-ionisation fraction, a Raman decay rate and the chi3-weighted density of several Kerr responses too. A table that never dips below zero is unchanged, a lifted one moves by at most 64 ulp of its largest value, about 70 times below the default fit tolerance of 1e-12, and a path that is negative, or changes sign by more than rounding, keeps the table it was fitted to; a density that goes further below zero is still refused.
