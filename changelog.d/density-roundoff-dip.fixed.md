A density path that touches zero no longer has its translation refused when the fitted table dips below zero by rounding: a curve meeting a zero end with zero slope, or an unresolved interval ringing about a vacuum, left the table about 1e-18 of its scale below zero, and the domain check refused the run. `profile_table` now lifts each dipping interval by its dip when every dip is within 64 ulp of the table's largest value; a path that goes further below zero is still refused.
